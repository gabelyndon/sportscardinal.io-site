I figured the first real post here should be an introduction, so you know who's on the other end of this.

## Who I am

My name is Gabe, and I'm the founder of SportsCardinal.io.

Before anything else: I'm a husband and a father of three. That comes first, and it shapes how I run this.

Professionally, I've spent close to two decades in oil and gas. I'm currently a Project Manager for a major midstream company here in Houston, Texas. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Interdisciplinary Studies from the University of Houston-Downtown, and an Associate of Applied Science in Process Technology from Lee College in Baytown.

That background probably sounds unrelated to sports cards, and on the surface it is. But project management in energy teaches you a few things that carry over well. You learn that schedules slip when nobody's accountable. You learn that shortcuts show up later, usually at the worst possible moment. And you learn that the people who do what they said they'd do are worth ten of the people who talk well.

I'm bringing that same standard here.

## How I want to do business

I'll keep this part short, because talk is cheap and you'll judge me on what I actually do.

My commitment is to run SportsCardinal with integrity and honesty, and above all with diligence and commitment. If I list a card, the description will be accurate. If I make a mistake, I'll own it and fix it. If I don't know something, I'll say so instead of guessing at your expense.

That's the whole policy. I don't think it needs to be more complicated than that.

## What I'm building

SportsCardinal has three pieces, and they're meant to work together.

**The blog.** What you're reading. My goal is to keep collectors current on what's actually happening in the hobby — grading, market movement, releases, the stuff that affects what your cards are worth and what you should do about it. New posts go up every Saturday.

**The game.** "Guess The Price of the Card" is the piece I've had the most fun building. You see a card, you guess what it's worth, you find out how close you were. It's entertainment first — but if you play it enough, it's also training. Reading comps is a skill, and like any skill it comes from reps. The game gives you reps without costing you money to get them wrong.

**The marketplace.** Cards for sale, at meaningfully lower fees than eBay. That's the pitch, and it's a straightforward one. eBay's cut is a real cost that gets passed to somebody, and I'd rather that somebody keep more of it.

I'm also planning to put together video on some of the pieces I have listed — walking through what a card is, what condition it's in, why it's priced where it's priced. Featured items and buy-it-now listings first. I think showing the card beats describing it.

## The bigger idea

What I want SportsCardinal to become is a community, not just a storefront.

The hobby has plenty of places to buy cards. It has fewer places where you can learn something, get entertained, and transact — with someone who's straight with you about all three. That's the gap I'm going after.

## Where things actually stand

Here's the honest part: this is a work in progress.

The game works and I'm proud of it. The blog starts now. The store is coming together. The videos are ahead of me, not behind me. Some of what I described above exists today and some of it is where I'm headed.

I'd rather tell you that plainly than pretend the whole thing is finished. You'll be able to watch it get built, and I think that's more interesting anyway.

If you've got feedback, ideas, or you just want to tell me something's broken, I want to hear it. Early input from people who actually collect is worth more to me right now than almost anything else.

Thanks for your time, and thanks for being here early.

— Gabe
