After years on GoDaddy Website Builder, this site now lives on its own — built from the ground up instead of a drag-and-drop template. The short version of why: control. We wanted a blog we could write in plain Markdown, a store that isn't locked into someone else's checkout flow, and room to build things a page builder just can't do — like a price-guessing game.

## What's here already

- **This blog**, published straight from a browser-based editor — no local setup required to post an update.
- **[Guess the Price](/game)**, a quick game where you look at a card, guess what it's worth, and see how close you got.

## What's coming

- A **store**, backed by Stripe Checkout, with the existing catalog migrated over from GoDaddy.
- Real pricing data behind the game, pulled from the same comps that price the store.

The domain hasn't moved yet — that's one of the last steps, once everything here is live and checked over. More soon.
